Advanced Glass Tech Could Minimise Display Creases on Future Samsung Foldables

via Android Authority2026-08-04

Samsung is reportedly developing new display technology aimed at solving one of the most persistent issues facing foldable smartphones. According to Android Authority, future Galaxy Z Fold devices could feature a redesigned ultra-thin glass layer engineered to significantly diminish the visibility of screen creases. This upcoming manufacturing advancement promises to deliver a much flatter and smoother inner screen experience for future foldable models.

While current foldable screens rely on flexible glass panels that show a slight depression along the hinge axis, Samsung's next-generation engineering aims to improve structural integrity. The middle display crease remains a primary hesitation for traditional smartphone users considering a jump to foldables. By making the inner display flatter and more durable, Samsung addresses the main cosmetic and durability concerns held by prospective buyers. These upcoming glass improvements bring foldables closer to matching the seamless glass feel of conventional smartphones while maintaining the benefits of a foldable screen.
