How Samsung Engineered a Massive Weight Reduction for Galaxy Z Fold 8

via GSMArena2026-09-09

Samsung has outlined the extensive engineering journey behind slimming down its latest book-style foldable phones, the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra. According to GSMArena, the standard Z Fold 8 weighs in at just 201 grams. That marks a dramatic decrease from the original Galaxy Fold, which weighed 276 grams, and the Z Fold 2 at 282 grams.

The company has systematically shaved weight from hinges, structural plates, and display layers across each successive release.

For enthusiasts of folding handsets, heavy hardware has long remained one of the biggest deterrents to daily adoption. Bringing a dual-screen device down to roughly 201 grams places it on equal footing with standard glass-slab flagships. Buyers can now access large internal displays without suffering from wrist strain or dealing with overly thick pockets during everyday carry.
