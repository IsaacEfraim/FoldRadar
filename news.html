Component Constraints May Cause Supply Shortages for Apple's Foldable Debut

via MacRumors2026-08-06

Apple's upcoming flagship lineup, including the highly anticipated foldable iPhone Ultra, could face severe supply shortages at launch time. Production bottlenecks surrounding critical memory components threaten to limit initial retail availability and lead to extended wait times during the early pre-order phase for these premium smartphones.

According to reporting from MacRumors, semiconductor analyst Tim Culpan revealed that Apple is struggling to secure sufficient DRAM supplies. Consequently, chip manufacturer TSMC has roughly $1 billion worth of unpackaged A20 Pro processors waiting on memory. These advanced chips are built on TSMC's 2nm process and utilize specialized Wafer-Level Multi-Chip Module packaging techniques.

For buyers eagerly awaiting Apple's first entry into the foldable phone market, these supply chain delays mean fast action will be necessary once pre-orders open. If DRAM shortages constrain early production, the foldable iPhone Ultra could sell out almost instantly, forcing interested customers to wait weeks or even months to receive their devices.
