Dual-Layer Glass Crease Solution Reportedly Heading to Android Foldables

via MacRumors2026-09-02

Apple's upcoming foldable design could reshape the entire market, as Android manufacturers prepare to mirror its screen engineering, according to MacRumors, citing a report from The Elec. Google, Oppo, and Vivo reportedly plan to adopt advanced ultra-thin glass configurations next year. This industry-wide transition follows Apple's expected unveiling of its book-style iPhone Ultra, which introduces a fresh approach to managing screen longevity.

Apple's approach reportedly utilizes two ultra-thin glass layers that sandwich the flexible display panel between them. This structural configuration isolates the fragile screen from making direct contact with the hinge beneath. By spreading mechanical stress across multiple layers of material, the engineering technique significantly enhances panel durability while reducing visible creasing along the folding axis over extended periods of daily use.

The visible crease remains one of the primary cosmetic and tactile complaints among foldable shoppers today. If Apple's dual-layer glass solution proves effective and Android rivals adopt the exact same architecture next year, customers will enjoy flatter, more resilient folding screens across the board. Improved durability should eliminate lingering hesitations for shoppers waiting for folding phone screens to feel just as flat as standard glass.
